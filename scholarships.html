Scholarships

Explore merit-based, need-based, special and early-bird scholarship programmes described by Zenith Educate.

Our Scholarship Programs

Designed to remove financial barriers and recognize talent and potential.

Merit-Based Scholarships

Awarded to students with excellent academic records or entrance test results. The original page states offers up to 100% fee waiver based on performance.

Need-Based Scholarships

For students from economically weaker backgrounds. Income proof and a short personal statement are required.

Special Scholarships

The original page lists support for female students, differently-abled candidates and children of defense personnel, with up to 50% discount.

Early Bird Scholarships

The original page describes up to 15% fee waiver for enrolment within the first 10 days of the admission cycle.

How to Apply

  1. Fill out the Scholarship Application Form during registration.
  2. Submit required documents such as ID, mark sheets and income certificate.
  3. Attend a scholarship test or interview, if required.

The original website notes that scholarships are batch-based and limited. Scholarship percentages and eligibility should be confirmed for the current admission cycle.
